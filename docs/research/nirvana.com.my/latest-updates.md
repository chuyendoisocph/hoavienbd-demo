# Nirvana “Latest Updates” — extraction notes

Source: `https://www.nirvana.com.my/resources/latest-updates/`

Captured references:

- `docs/design-references/nirvana.com.my/latest-updates-desktop.png` — 1440 × 900 viewport.
- `docs/design-references/nirvana.com.my/latest-updates-desktop-full.png` — long desktop capture.
- `docs/design-references/nirvana.com.my/latest-updates-mobile.png` — 390 px mobile capture.

The page is used only as a layout and interaction reference. Malaysian branding, people, campaign content and images are not reused. The Hoa Viên implementation uses existing, locally stored images from Hoa Viên Bình Dương.

## Visual extraction

- Hero: full-width image below a fixed white header, centered white title and subtitle, dark image overlay.
- Primary news area: saturated indigo background, centered heading/intro, one large visual story card.
- Secondary campaign area: white background, spacious editorial layout, image and content presented side-by-side on desktop.
- Events CTA: two columns, large photographic panel paired with oversized indigo display text and contact action.
- Video area: compact centered heading and wide media panel.
- Desktop content width is approximately 1080–1200 px; mobile collapses every split section to one column.

## Hoa Viên adaptation

- Route: `/su-kien`.
- Hero uses a real aerial Hoa Viên image.
- The blue feature panel becomes “Sự kiện nổi bật”.
- The campaign area becomes “Hoạt động thường niên”.
- The events CTA becomes a visit/contact prompt using the existing hotline and consultation flow.
- Exact Gregorian dates are intentionally omitted until they are confirmed. Recurring occasions use their lunar-calendar labels.
